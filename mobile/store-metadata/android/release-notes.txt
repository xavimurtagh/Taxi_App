Welcome to OpenRide v1.0.0 — the launch of community-owned ride sharing!

What's new:
- Request rides with transparent, fair pricing
- Real-time GPS tracking during rides
- Secure payments via Stripe with full fare breakdowns
- Driver mode with instant and weekly payout options
- Community governance — vote on platform decisions
- Public transparency dashboard for platform finances
- In-app messaging and masked phone calls
- Peer review dispute resolution system
- Referral program to grow the OpenRide community
- Scheduled rides for planning ahead
- Ride history and digital receipts

OpenRide is a non-profit cooperative. Drivers keep 90-95% of every fare. No hidden fees. No data selling. Your ride, your community, your platform.
